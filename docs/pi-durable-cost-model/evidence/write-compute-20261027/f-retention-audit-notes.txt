Recommendation
Retain F as a bounded removal of unnecessary computation/transient writes and
continue the final cross-engine/append/reference cohort. This recommendation
accepts explicitly disclosed small sampled-memory increases and unresolved
timing variability; it is not an all-metric nonregression verdict. Correctness
checks, exact request/reopen evidence, deterministic write reduction and the
mixed confirmation results support this limited decision. Do not describe F
as an established global CPU/latency improvement or Pi Durable parity. Final
retention/reporting must include the final cohort results and these negatives.
